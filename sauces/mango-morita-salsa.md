# Mango Morita Salsa

## Ingredients

+ 1-2 Alfonso or Manilla mangos
+ 1 dried Morita chile
+ 1/2 yellow onion
+ cilantro (4-5 stalks with leaves)
+ 4 cloves garlic
+ 5 roma tomatoes (I've also tried jackpot and early girls with success, don't use cluster / vine tomatoes they did not work well!)
+ salt
+ lemon (optional if needed)


## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to @400F
2. Setup a sheet pan with a baking sheet.
3. Slice tomatoes in half and place cut-side down.
4. Peel and dice mangos, put onto the pan.
5. Chop yellow onion into just 2 chunks (i.e. 2 quarters of a whole onion).
6. Drizzle everything with oil and salt.
7. Peel the garlic cloves and wrap into aluminum foil with some oil and salt inside. (This roasts/confits the garlic without it burning)
8. Place everything into the oven, use multiple pans if needed.
9. Let roast for 20-25 minutes.
10. Heat an pan and toast the dried morita chile directly on the pan (no oil needed). After 30sec-1min it should start getting soft. Move it to a small bowl with hot water to let it soak while everything is cooking in the oven.
11. Once the tomatoes are all roasted on top, let it cool slightly and remove the tomato skins. They should be easy to just pluck off with tweezers or tongs.
12. Put everything into a blender or food processor with the handful of cilantro. Slice open and de-seed the morita chile before adding to the blender (or not depending on the spice level desired).
13. Season to taste with salt and add lemon if it seems to need more of an acidic kick (depending on the tomatoes, this might not be necessary).
