# Pesto

## Ingredients
+ 1 cup basil
+ 1 clove garlic
+ .25 cup pine nuts
+ .25 cup grated parmesan cheese
+ .5 cup Extra Virgin Olive Oil (California Ranch Global Blend) 
+ Salt

## Instructions
1. Peel garlic clove and grind with salt in mortar.
2. Lightly toast pine nuts
3. Mash in pine nuts with garlic.
