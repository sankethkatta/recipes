# Tofu Tacos

## Ingredients

+ [Tofu for Tacos](/mains/tofu-for-tacos.md)
+ [Mango Morita Salsa](/sauces/mango-morita-salsa.md)
+ cheddar cheese
+ Tortillas (guerrero riquisimas)

## Instructions

1. Heat a pan, add a tortilla, then shredded cheese and wait until melted.
2. Spoon on the "Tofu for Tacos" and a dallop of "Mango Morita Salsa"
3. Enjoy!
