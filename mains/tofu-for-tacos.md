# Tofu for Tacos

## Ingredients

+ 1 block firm tofu (house foods red box)
+ 4 cremini mushrooms
+ 1/2 red onion
+ 2 corn cobs
+ Salt
+ Taco Seasoning (morton & basset)

## Instructions

1. Cube the tofu and soak it in salted water for ~15 minutes. (Not required, but helps get some moisture out and seasons the tofu)
2. Slice the red onion and the cremini mushrooms thinly.
3. De-husk and slice kernels off the corn.
4. On a large pan, add oil and roast the onions and mushrooms with some salt and taco seasoning. After ~5 minutes you should see the onions start to brown and soften. If they are still hard to the bite, keep roasting and add more oil as necessary to not let them dry out completely.
5. Remove the onion and mushroom from the pan and repeat with the corn until kernels start to brown ~2-3 minutes.
6. Finally, drain the tofu, roast in the pan with some oil. Add salt and taco seasoning as needed. Leave without moving for ~1 min to allow browning.
7. Mix it all together and season as needed.
